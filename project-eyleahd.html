Suzanne Valadon and Edgar Degas | Eyleahd
ROLE | ANIMATION DIRECTOR
I was the animation director for this project, directed by @biancaaleal, produced by @theyouth_company, and painted by the best team @atelietrintaenove ⚡️

Countless frames were painted, photographed, printed, and painted all over again to match the texture of Edgar Degas's work, who shifted his techniques over the years due to a degenerative disease that affected his vision.
