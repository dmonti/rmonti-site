The Wave | AstraZeneca
ROLE | DIRECTOR
This animated video uses an illustrated "wave" and woman to demonstrate how a cancer patient can feel overwhelmed with a "rush" of information and emotion upon diagnosis of breast cancer.

As she navigates through her cancer diagnosis, information about her cancer, like receiving a BRA test, helps her move through the wave and swim to the surface, giving her the power to take back control of her choices and understand her treatment possibilities.

The craft and detail put into the animation is our tribute to the amount of care and thought put into every step of a patient's cancer journey. Each scene began as a hand painted watercolor drawing, brought to life through animation that conveys the emotion that can come their search for the right treatment.
