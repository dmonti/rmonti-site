Hello, I'm Rafael Monti.

I'm a Brazilian Animation Director and Motion Designer from Minas Gerais, the famous land of pão de queijo and coffee, which pretty much explains my two biggest passions.

I hold a degree in Animation Design from UNIVILLE (University of the Joinville Region) and have been working in the industry since 2013. Over the years, I have had the honor of winning several awards alongside great friends and colleagues, including The One Show, ADC Awards, D&AD, Clio Music, Clio, LIA, Ciclope, El Ojo, and the one I'm most proud of: a Gold Lion in Film Craft & Animation at the Cannes Lions.
