About

Hello, I'm Rafael.

I'm Latin American, Brazilian, and from Minas Gerais, the famous state of 'cheese bread' and coffee, which explains why they're two of my biggest addictions.

I hold a degree in Animation Design from UNIVILLE (University of the Joinville Region). I've been working in the field since 2013, and throughout my career I've had the honor of winning several awards alongside colleagues and friends, including The One Show, ADC Awards, D&AD, Clio for Music, Clio, LIA, Ciclope, El Ojo and the one I'm most proud of, a Gold Lion in Film Craft & Animation at Cannes Lions.

For more information, feel free to follow me at @r_montis
