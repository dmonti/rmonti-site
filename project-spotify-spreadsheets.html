Spreadbeats | Spotify
ROLE | ANIMATION DIRECTOR
Ever since I started working with animation, I've had this idea of someday turning a simple spreadsheet into an animation, but I never imagined it would be for Spotify. It was a crazy and super fun challenge. Nobody really knew what the final look would be like, and even though there was a company (Uncharted Limbo Collective) working on translating the film into the .xlsx file during the project, we were constantly testing and validating if what we were creating would actually work once converted into the real format.

In partnership with Colossal, Uncharted Limbo Collective, and FCB NY, Spotify crafted and coded this 10MB file, transforming ordinary spreadsheet functions into an immersive storytelling experience.

We worked with ASCII Art, Pixel Art, and even 4x1 pixel art. We literally asked illustrators to create artwork directly inside Excel.
